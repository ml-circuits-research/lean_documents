Implementation review · 4 November 2026

Audit the finding and its argument separately

The reusable method takes a supplied dossier and report, reconstructs relevant facts and rules, chooses verification methods, and tests each conclusion and each material argument obligation. A separate brief, a particular document converter and a domain-specific template are not mandatory.

From sources to independent obligations

  1. Preserve originals and report passages
  2. Extract attributed facts, rules and unknowns
  3. Infer criteria and choose methods
  4. Check conclusion and argument obligations
  5. Explain results and coverage

A fact map keeps the source identity, exact span, speaker, epistemic role, polarity, units, time, version, applicability, exceptions and uncertainty. A quoted assertion remains attributed testimony unless there is a reason to adopt it as a premise. The method card records the question, required facts and rules, support/refutation target, consistency checks, and what Lean can actually establish.

Methods may combine arithmetic, sets, temporal constraints, state transitions, predicates, quantified propositions and countermodels. Statistical or causal findings need their own justified assumptions. The agent selects a suitable formalization from the claim and dossier; it does not force every domain into a Boolean classification table.

Four separate checks: Does the dossier justify the conclusion? Are the report's premises true or supported? Does the inference follow from those premises? Are its citations and rules applicable? A failure in one question does not automatically decide the others.

The optional EvidenceLogic vocabulary defines support and refutation with a consistency witness, and underdetermination with two admissible worlds that disagree on the claim. It uses only core Lean. It does not parse documents or certify a source interpretation.

Lean-only audit: a correct conclusion with a bad premise

The source records amount 9 and defines within budget as at most 10. The report says the item is within budget “because every positive amount is within budget”; it also claims the item is over budget and approved. The dossier leaves approval unconstrained.

FindingIndependent resultFormal explanation
Within budget, based on positive-amount sufficiencyConclusion supported; offered universal premise refuted.9 ≤ 10 establishes the conclusion. Amount 11 refutes the proposed rule. Modus ponens would be valid if that rule were true; the rule is the defect.
Over budgetRefuted under the disclosed numeric meaning.The nonempty evidence model entails within budget, hence contradicts over budget.
ApprovedInsufficient support; formally underdetermined.Two amount-9 worlds agree with the dossier and disagree on approval. This does not establish actual nonapproval.

Exact Lean target assertions, axiom output, shell replay and source hashes were executed; source interpretation and claim coverage are separately recorded agent reviews. Copied-skill workflow

A separate audit used a copy of the skill folder, a new HTML stock register, and a report with four conclusions. No review brief, repository examples, fixture answers or original-package Python imports were needed. The built-in adapter could not read HTML; the agent inspected the original and imported nine anchored segments with provenance and review acceptances.

  • Counted stock 45: supported, with a sufficient author argument.
  • Counted stock 60: refuted; the addition is correct but the population premise is false.
  • A is eligible for release: supported; the offered signature-only rule is false, as shown by signed but quarantined B.
  • A never received prior clearance: undetermined; the inference from missing records fails, with two admissible historical states.

Remaining boundaries

There is no fixed dossier domain, number of sources or report template. There are still finite resource budgets and potentially unreadable documents or undefined concepts. These lead to explicit coverage gaps or blocked formalization. The agent supplies semantic interpretation; Lean checks the resulting formal proposition. This review removes avoidable implementation restrictions, not the need to inspect source-to-model fidelity.

Agent-native execution has fewer automated metadata gates unless the agent supplies equivalent tooling. That difference is disclosed, not hidden behind a generic success label. The synthetic validations demonstrate specific working routes; they do not prove that every future document or library will work.
