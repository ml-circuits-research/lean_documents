## Concrete issue found and correction verified

**IR-01 — Unsupported contents of an absent annex were initially called contradicted (corrected).** In the initial inspected mixed dossier 11 finding `F11-07`, reasoning said “The claimed annex confirmation is contradicted,” and the premise “the listed reliability annex confirms the absence of incidents” was marked `contradicted`. The source `examples/11-report-derived-criteria/sources/packet-register.md` K-02 states that the annex is not supplied and that the packet contains no later observations. It says nothing that refutes the unseen annex's actual contents. K-01 limits supplied records to day 20. Therefore the premise about unseen contents is unsupported; K-02 cannot serve as confirmation. The zero-incidents conclusion correctly remained undetermined throughout.

The coordinator was notified before this review finished. Reinspection verified the corrected `F11-07`: the annex-content premise is now `unsupported`; the reasoning explicitly says its contents are unavailable; and the citation assessment is confined to rejecting K-02 as the claimed confirmation, without refuting unseen annex contents. The retained post-correction proof result records `passed`, checked at `2026-11-04T16:58:55.167605+00:00`. This correction changes a material argument assessment even though it leaves the conclusion verdict unchanged. It demonstrates why agreement with expected conclusion labels alone is not a sufficient quality measure.

No further material conclusion error was established within the review scope. This is a bounded review result, not a guarantee that every possible semantic or extraction error has been excluded.

## Breadth and remaining limits

The suite exercises a useful range of reasoning failures: true and false finite claims, explicit counterexamples, incomplete conjunctions, alternate routes, false source attribution, circular support, conflicting testimony, exact arithmetic and units, bounded absence, unknown observations, time/version scope, and extraction uncertainty. Dossier 11 adds a particularly important independently supported conclusion with an invalid supplied argument. Both variants of every dossier were actually audited separately; the matrix in `case-coverage.json` separates those executions from mechanism tests and design-only rules.

This is not coverage of every domain or document type. The packets are invented and often explicitly explain completeness, missing evidence, and the intended distinction. That is appropriate for controlled regression cases but easier than an uncurated client archive. No representative accuracy estimate, calibrated probabilistic confidence, external legal correctness, medical judgment, or real operational safety certification follows. Native PDF/DOCX/OCR examples do not establish general handwriting, multilingual, damaged-scan, or arbitrary-layout accuracy. Source authenticity and actual cryptographic signature verification remain outside executed domain verification.

Coverage is also finer than a category checkbox. Procurement tests final rounding but do not provide a selected report claim at an exact half-cent tie. Incident response defines unioning overlapping pauses, while the selected incident uses two nonoverlapping pauses. Those rule branches are present in design but are not established as independently exercised dossier cases here. Empty source dossiers are now exercised both by mechanism tests and the separately identified coordinator-authored dossier 12 below. Dependency invalidation and bounded ingestion have executed mechanism tests and retained artifacts; a normative report claim about those mechanisms must not be counted as their execution.
