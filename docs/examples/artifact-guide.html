How to read an evidence-backed audit

The product audits findings in supplied reports against supplied documents. It does not generate the original substantive report. Read the claim, decision criteria, source evidence, applicable rules, assumptions, reasoning, and limits together.

Input snapshots and manifests

input/report*, input/sources/, input/originals/, manifest.json, input/manifest.json

Identify the exact selected report and supplied evidence; hashes bind bytes and native originals remain downloadable.

Hashes do not authenticate a document or certify its factual truth. The alternate report is never evidence for the selected report.

Claim registry

claims.json, input/report-ledger.json

Record report anchors, registered claims, and (v2) anchor classifications and premise/citation/inference spans.

Legacy v1 registries do not satisfy the v2 coverage contract merely because old proofs pass.

Extraction and provenance

ingestion.json, extraction/, extraction-lock.json, input/source-ledger.json, source-ledger.jsonl

Retain extracted passages, document/page/coordinate provenance where available, uncertainty, and extraction review.

Extraction is not source authentication. OCR uncertainty, tables, and layout require review against native bytes.

Disk index and retrieval

input/index.sqlite3, search-history.jsonl, search-ledger.json, search-records/, retrieval-scope.json

Preserve indexed corpus and recorded search scope, queries, and inspected passages. SQLite is downloadable.

A search log is not proof that every relevant real-world record exists in the supplied corpus.

Criteria and argument audit

findings.json: criteria, argument_audit, support_search

Expose operational questions, decision criteria, source rules, separate premise/citation/inference assessments, and independent conclusion reasoning.

Missing support is a support gap, not proof of a false underlying claim. Historical records can lack these structured v2 fields.

Model and correspondence

models/*.lean, model-notes.md, findings.json: formalization

Show definitions, source transcription, assumptions, theorem statements, and explanations linking the model to the finding.

Lean checks the encoded statement; source-to-model fidelity and the adequacy of chosen assumptions require semantic review.

Executed target checks and axioms

checks/*.lean, logs/target-*.log, logs/lake-build.log, proof-results.json

Show actual recorded execution status, explicit target checks, theorem names, model hashes, and reported axioms.

A successful build alone does not establish that the target matches the report claim. An auxiliary theorem may prove only a limited support gap or countermodel.

Environment and original exports

lean-toolchain, lakefile.toml, lake-manifest.json, environment.lock, export-manifest.json

Record toolchain requirements and original export integrity metadata; these files are copied unchanged.

Publication does not rerun Lean or modify an original export manifest. Toolchain binaries and build caches are intentionally excluded.

Semantic review and coverage

semantic-review.md, independent-review/, coverage.md, audit-report.md

Expose human/agent review findings, unresolved issues, exclusions, argument findings, and reported coverage.

An executed proof is not an independent semantic review. A review file is a recorded assessment, not a guarantee of completeness.

Revision and fresh reproduction

history/, attempts/, invalidation.json; separate reproduction snapshots

Preserve failed attempts, changes, stale-dependency information and separately recorded later rechecks when available.

A later proof recheck does not retroactively upgrade a legacy registry to v2 or repeat a blind domain audit.

Publication integrity and omissions

Publication SHA-256 inventory records every snapshot source and every generated file. Original audit/export manifests are retained untouched. A publication hash establishes byte identity with the copied artifact; it is not a new audit or proof check. Toolchain binaries, build caches, symlinks, and fixture authoring masters are excluded. Native originals and intermediate indexes are retained.

Missing and partial coverage

Only artifacts present in the supplied bundles are shown. No missing criterion, argument audit, provenance coordinate, proof, review, or failed attempt is reconstructed as though it had been executed. Legacy results and fresh rechecks remain separately labeled. Evaluator expectations are available only in the explicitly marked study area.

Static study edition · works from file:// · no network or JavaScript required. Sources, audits, and evaluator expectations have different roles.
