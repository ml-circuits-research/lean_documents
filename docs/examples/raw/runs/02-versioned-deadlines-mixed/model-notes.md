# Model interpretation

`Audit.lean` uses core Std and ordinary finite natural-number computations. `Case` encodes one named register case; the finite domain is the four concrete constants c201-c204. It does not quantify over unknown cases. All dates are after the origin, so natural minute offsets are sufficient; subtraction and negative dates are unnecessary. Timestamp facts are source transcriptions, not authenticity guarantees.

The origin is 2028-02-20 00:00 UTC. `receiptDay` is the Gregorian date offset, `receiptMinute` is minute of that day, `submission` is absolute minutes from origin. `at17 d = d*1440+1020`. C01 maps to c201=(day 0, minute 540, standard, submission at17 10). C02 maps to c202=(day 9, minute 1439, standard, submission at17 19 + 1). C03 maps to c203=(day 10, minute 0, standard, submission at17 17). C04 maps to c204=(day 11, minute 600, restricted, submission day 21 at minute 960). Assignment times are excluded from revision calculation because V1-01 and V2-01 explicitly exclude them, not because they were overlooked. T01 records the export label's different meaning.

Manual date mapping: leap-year February 20=day 0, February 29=day 9, March 1=day 10, March n=day n+9. Independent executed Python Gregorian datetime calculations in logs/calendar-transcription.log confirm every used timestamp. Receipt day zero plus ten gives D-201 March 1 and D-202 March 10; plus seven gives D-203 March 8. Gregorian date conversion is outside Lean: the arithmetic is checked in Lean, while its calendar correspondence is manually and computationally cross-checked here. No weekend or holiday rollover is modeled because B02/V1-02/V2-02 expressly exclude one.

`revision` implements V1-01/V2-01 using accepted-receipt minute < 14400 for revision 1 and otherwise revision 2. `standardDue` implements V1-02/V2-02 only when standard=true, and returns None for restricted cases under V1-03/V2-03. None means unavailable Annex R information, not no deadline. `timely` is the inclusive submission ≤ deadline comparison. All constants are witnessed register cases, so direct proofs are not vacuous universal claims.

Direct theorem correspondence: version201 and deadline201 support VD-01a/b; timely201 supports VD-02; version202Refuted refutes VD-03 while identifying revision 1; deadline202 supports VD-04 despite the report's incorrect version rationale; timely202Refuted refutes VD-05 by one-minute lateness; version203 and timely203 support VD-06a/b; deadline203Refuted refutes VD-07 and gives the March 8 alternative. The complete exact targets are in findings.json and are checked by the harness, including axioms.

`restrictedDeadlineOpen` is auxiliary for VD-08 and VD-09. It uses hypothetical final deadlines at day 18 (March 9 17:00) and day 22 (March 13 17:00). Since no supplied source gives Annex R duration, calendar basis, or pause criteria, neither duration violates a supplied restricted rule. These are possible completions of absent normative data, not assertions of Annex R's content. Both keep the same observed case, request and submission; neither requires claiming a pause was approved or rejected. They show that one deadline equals the report's date while the other differs, and that submission timeliness flips. The theorem establishes only these concrete countermodel comparisons and None from the standard rule; the correspondence judgment that both completions remain open is documentary, not a formal completeness proof of arbitrary absent regulations. No theorem refutes the actual restricted-case deadline or timeliness.

Fairness (VD-10) has no operational definition or population in T03, so it has no formal target. The informal attributed statement is preserved without promoting it to an unconditional fact. No closed-world assumption about unrecorded events is used. The search was exhaustive over supplied paragraphs only. Independent semantic review and export are assigned to the parent and are not claimed here. Lean execution is authorized by the repository instructions despite the original fixture's prospective-only B04 sentence.
