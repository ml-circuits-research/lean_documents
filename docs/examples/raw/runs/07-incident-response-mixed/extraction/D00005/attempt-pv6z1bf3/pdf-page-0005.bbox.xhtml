[M04] The completion certificate states that the operator knew of B during the maintenance period and informed the coordinator. That contact is not the human acknowledgement specified by the playbook, which was delivered later and is recorded in the incident timeline. The authorization does not characterize B as a false alarm or remove it from the incident register. An analyst must therefore retain its accepted detection timestamp, use the specific permitted deferred clock start, and compare the actual delivered acknowledgement against the resulting deadline. Counting from the original detection while ignoring the authorized deferral would apply the wrong effective clock, even though that original detection remains a true event.
