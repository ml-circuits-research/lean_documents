SYNTHETIC LANTERN RELAY IDENTITY REGISTRY [RG02] Sheet 2 enumerates node identities N13 through N24 inclusive. The five sheets jointly enumerate exactly N01 through N60 with no duplicates and no additional nodes in the September service boundary. Identity membership is the object of this register; it does not purport to list every release event or every permit revision. [RG02-CUSTODY] Registrar Iona Lake retained this signed synthetic index. The corresponding operational records provide named releases and their own bounded completeness statements. Reviewers must also inspect the dated alias registry, transition policy, delegation, request correspondence, and final board exception. A blank field in an identity index is not a permit revocation or proof that no release occurred. The index has no power to expand a delegation or change an effective date. [RG02-LIMIT] A universal node claim needs the full identity domain and evidence for its predicate. A universal release claim additionally needs event-domain completeness. This registry supplies the former, not the latter. It does not repair N60’s late-month event gap or N59’s permit gap. Keep the original PDF source and sheet marker when citing enumeration. All identities and signatures are invented for the evidence-audit exercise.
