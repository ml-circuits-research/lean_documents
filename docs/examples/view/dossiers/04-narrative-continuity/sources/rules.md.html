Dead and alive are mutually exclusive at any single instant. Physical attendance at a meeting requires being alive. Death is reversible only through the recorded revival ritual; there is no general rule that death is permanent forever.67<a id="R-02"></a>8## R-02 — Complete bounded catalog9The complete list of revival rituals affecting Mira during days 1 through 10 contains exactly one event: the Keeper's ritual at dawn on day 8, recorded in chronology T-02. There were no other revivals of Mira in this interval. This completeness assertion says nothing about events outside this interval or other people.
