1# Synthetic North Quay stock rules23[R01] Unit definitions and boundary. The inventory boundary consists of rooms W1 and W2 and the fenced return cage at the invented North Quay warehouse. The cutoff is 18:00 on 14 April 2032. All quantities are exact integer individual sleeves, called units. A sealed full crate contains twelve units. A sealed full carton contains six units. A pallet has no fixed conversion. A partial-container label overrides the standard full-container quantity; an empty container contributes zero. All names and transactions in this packet are invented. These rules define the finite exercise and do not prescribe real shipping procedures.45[R02] Movement semantics. Opening stock plus accepted inbound movements minus completed outbound movements plus accepted returns determines book on-hand. A printed ticket, order, reservation, or pick request changes no physical inventory merely by existing. Departure occurs at the signed actual custody handoff across the boundary. Customer delivery is a separate later event. A carrier can hold inventory that has already departed but is not yet delivered. Return increases on-hand when the receiving custodian accepts the goods back across the boundary. Returned goods in the fenced cage are inside the boundary even before reshelving. An accepted return is a separate movement, not a reason to erase the original departure.67[R03] Lot identity and availability. Lots A, B, C, and D are separate even though their sleeves share a catalog description. An excess in one lot cannot repair a deficit in another. Available stock is book on-hand minus quarantined units minus separately reserved units, with no overlapping deductions. The A quarantine contains seven units. The B reservation contains eight different units. Quarantine and reservation change internal status without transferring custody. Physical presence, book balance, and available quantity are different predicates. A physical count may disagree with a journal-derived balance, and the disagreement must remain visible rather than silently redefining one quantity to match the other.89[R04] Evidence scope. Signed actual transfer receipts take precedence over unsigned planned quantities for determining completed custody movements. The journal and register are complete for A and B movements only within the stated day. No such completeness applies to D. Equal-authority physical counts of the same boundary and cutoff remain competing evidence unless a recount or authorized correction resolves them. A later filename does not give precedence. A pale or obscured numeral in a scan requires visual review and corroboration. An OCR guess is not a signed quantity. If available evidence does not establish a unique accepted amount, report the missing argument without inventing a number or asserting that the shipment necessarily failed.
