1<a id="docx-p-0001"></a>2Harbor Procurement Standard HPS: operative clauses34<a id="docx-p-0002"></a>5All organizations, people, agreements, events, and rules in this source are synthetic.67<a id="docx-p-0003"></a>8[P01]910<a id="docx-p-0004"></a>11Harbor Research Cooperative purchases components for an offshore research laboratory. This standard distinguishes a requisition, an approval, an order commitment, delivery acceptance, an invoice, and payment. A requisition records a proposed need. An approval authorizes a specified transaction within the approving person's authority. An order commitment binds Harbor to purchase. Delivery acceptance confirms receipt or technical acceptance. An invoice requests payment. None of these events automatically establishes that any other event occurred. The assessment period is 1 May through 30 June 2029, and all times in this standard and the transaction records are UTC. No external procurement law forms part of this fictional agreement.1213<a id="docx-p-0005"></a>14[P02]1516<a id="docx-p-0006"></a>17The applicable competitive-purchase threshold is inclusive: a normalized purchase value equal to the threshold requires competition unless an established exception applies. Revision 1 uses EUR 20,000.00. Revision 2 uses EUR 25,000.00. Below the applicable threshold, this standard does not require competitive solicitation, although a valid order approval is still required. Competition means inviting at least three independent qualified suppliers, giving each the same specification, recording the responses or documented nonresponses, and signing a comparison before order commitment. A supplier's quote, a market-price screenshot, or an existing relationship is not by itself the prescribed competition. A competition certificate expressly marked complete attests that these steps occurred; a quotation folder without that certificate does not automatically attest them.1819<a id="docx-p-0007"></a>20[P03]2122<a id="docx-p-0008"></a>23Two or more purchases aggregate when they concern the same funded project, the same supplier legal entity, and components of one operational requirement committed within an inclusive thirty-calendar-date window. The first commitment date is day one, and the last included date is first date plus twenty-nine calendar days. Different order numbers, cost-center labels, delivery pallets, or invoice dates do not split an otherwise common operational requirement. Purchases do not aggregate merely because the suppliers have similar names or serve the same laboratory. A documented change to a genuinely independent operational requirement can rebut aggregation, but relabeling a single installation cannot. For an aggregate, the competitive threshold applies to the combined normalized value before the first commitment if the full requirement was already known then.2425<a id="docx-p-0009"></a>26[P04]2728<a id="docx-p-0010"></a>29Normalized purchase value is an exact euro amount rounded once to euro cents after the following calculation. Add all unavoidable consideration for the requirement, including freight and installation. Subtract only unconditional rebates already binding at commitment. Exclude separately stated recoverable VAT. Convert the resulting transaction-currency amount using the fixed contractual exchange rate in the approved requisition schedule; this rate, not a later market quote, controls. Carry the conversion as an exact rational number, then round to the nearest cent, with exact half-cent ties rounded upward for these nonnegative amounts. Do not round each intermediate component, truncate fractional cents, round down to a whole euro, or test the threshold against the unrounded result. The synthetic contracts permit fractional transaction-currency cents, so an amount with three decimal places is not automatically a transcription error.3031<a id="docx-p-0011"></a>32[P05]3334<a id="docx-p-0012"></a>35A purchase requires a written approval identifying the order, value, and supplier, granted strictly before commitment by a person whose authority covers the value and category at the approval instant. Authority limits are inclusive unless a delegation says otherwise. The procurement director has standing authority for all monetary values in this dossier. A delegated manager has only the category, amount, and time range expressly stated in the delegation. An approval at the delegation's inclusive expiry instant is valid if all other conditions hold; a later commitment does not itself invalidate that approval. An approval granted after expiry is not rescued by the fact that the requisition was submitted earlier. Manager approval and completion of competitive solicitation are separate requirements: satisfying one does not imply the other.3637<a id="docx-p-0013"></a>38[P06]3940<a id="docx-p-0014"></a>41The urgent-safety exception waives only competitive solicitation. It requires a confirmed active safety incident, a director's written emergency declaration identifying the affected requirement strictly before the first commitment, and a director's retrospective review completed at or after that commitment and no later than forty-eight hours afterward. Exactly forty-eight hours is within the limit. An urgent email or a genuine incident alone is insufficient. The declaration must describe the risk addressed by the purchase; mere inconvenience, deferred research, or an expiring quote does not establish the incident condition. A separately valid order approval remains necessary. Neither an emergency declaration nor a later review is automatically an order approval unless the same signed instrument expressly contains both decisions.4243<a id="docx-p-0015"></a>44[P07]4546<a id="docx-p-0016"></a>47The proprietary-compatibility exception may waive competition when the required component has an only compatible source within the meaning of Annex Q. Annex Q supplies the governing compatibility test, required certification, and treatment of technically workable substitute components. This operative clause does not say whether compatibility is determined by physical interoperability, warranty certification, or another test. Evidence that one vendor is the sole certified vendor does not automatically resolve the unspecified Annex Q standard. Annex Q is a normative attachment, and its absence does not mean its conditions are waived, satisfied, or impossible to satisfy. The dossier's declaration that an attachment is missing concerns the supplied package, not every copy that might exist elsewhere.4849<a id="docx-p-0017"></a>50[P08]5152<a id="docx-p-0018"></a>53The standard defines compliance as the conjunction of the applicable competition requirement or a fully established competition exception, and valid pre-commitment order approval. The dossier's explicitly complete transaction register may establish event absence within its named coverage. Other files, including incoming correspondence and partial scans, have no general completeness status. Conflicting signed attestations remain attributable statements until an authorized correction or adjudication resolves them. Signature authentication establishes who signed, not that the statement is necessarily true. The standard supplies no metric for describing a transaction as prudent, fair, strategically wise, or commercially elegant. Those descriptions are separate judgments rather than consequences of a threshold calculation.54
