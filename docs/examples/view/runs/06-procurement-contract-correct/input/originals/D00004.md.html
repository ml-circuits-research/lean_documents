1# Delegation notice and authority register23All organizations, people, agreements, events, and rules in this source are synthetic.45<a id="D01"></a>6## [D01]78The procurement director delegates authority to manager Dana Cole for laboratory consumables with normalized purchase value no greater than EUR 15,000.00 per independent requirement. The authority begins at 2029-06-01 00:00 UTC and expires at 2029-06-15 17:00 UTC, inclusive. The amount limit is inclusive. The delegation does not cover instrument acquisitions, emergency declarations, competition waivers, or approval of an aggregate whose combined requirement exceeds the limit. Dana may approve qualifying purchases during this interval even if operational commitment follows after expiry, provided approval still strictly precedes commitment and no approval condition is violated. The authority is measured when the approval is granted, not when a request enters Dana's inbox.910<a id="D02"></a>11## [D02]1213The authority register is complete for Dana's delegation and amendments during June 2029. It contains no extension, renewal, oral-authority confirmation, substitute delegation, or suspension. The stated expiry therefore remains controlling. This authority completeness is deliberately narrow: it does not purport to list every procurement approval by every employee. P-15 and P-16 approval events are established by their transaction register entries, while the director's standing authority comes from P05. The laboratory team may continue using Dana as a contact person after expiry, but a person's presence in a directory or continued employment does not renew a formal monetary delegation.1415<a id="D03"></a>16## [D03]1718An administrative reminder sent to the purchasing team on June 14 says that the last authorized minute is the minute displayed as 17:00 UTC on June 15. It expressly excludes approvals displayed as 17:01 UTC or later. The timestamps in this dossier are stipulated exact minute events, not uncertain minute-long intervals; neither an implied grace period nor an unrecorded earlier click should be assumed. The reminder also separates value authority from timing authority. An amount exactly at EUR 15,000.00 satisfies the monetary ceiling, but satisfaction of that ceiling does not compensate for a late approval. Conversely, approval at the expiry instant is not late merely because the first second of a later commitment occurs afterward.1920<a id="D04"></a>21## [D04]2223The director's standing authority includes instruments and consumables above Dana's limit, but the director has not delegated emergency-waiver decisions to Dana. A routine manager signature on a purchase order therefore cannot establish the urgent-safety declaration or its retrospective review. Documents combining several decisions must name each decision expressly. A reviewer can assess a valid approval and a missing competition certificate independently, because neither is a substitute for the other. The register's declaration that P-16 has no alternative approval is an event statement in the explicit coverage L01, not an inference from the fact that the folder happened to contain only Dana's signature page.
