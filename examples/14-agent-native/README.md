# Is this expense within budget?

A short record gives one expense and its budget limit. Check both the report’s answer and the reason it gives.

**Question:** Does the recorded expense fit the budget, and what is known about approval?

Upload a three-statement spending record and one report. Choose either [the careful report](report-correct.md) or [the report with deliberate errors](report-mixed.md) and audit it separately against all files in [sources/](sources/). No separate brief is needed; derive the questions from the report and source rules.

The audit can check a simple amount comparison, a report’s general premise and the limits of the approval evidence. It reviews the supplied report, including its reasons and citations; it does not write the original report.

The record says nothing about approval. A valid budget calculation cannot establish approval on its own.

These are fictional examples. Keep this guide, the alternate report, metadata, generation files and evaluator material outside the audit input. Evaluator expectations are withheld during a blind audit; the selected report and source documents are always available.

Historical execution records are indexed under [validation/runs](../../validation/runs/). This guide does not constitute a new validation run. Original reports and sources retain their bytes so they can be matched to prior results. New runs belong under `tmp/audit-runs/`, with a separate directory for each report.
