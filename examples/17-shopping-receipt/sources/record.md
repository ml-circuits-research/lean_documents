# Shopping receipt and till rules

[S1] This fictional receipt lists exactly three purchases: bread 300 cents, milk 200 cents and apples 400 cents. Prices are final; no tax, discount or other line applies.
[S2] The purchase total is the sum of the three listed prices. A customer offering a 1000-cent cash note would be due change equal to 1000 minus the purchase total. This is a hypothetical cash calculation, not a record that cash was handed over.
[S3] This packet contains no payment-terminal result, bank record or confirmation of a completed payment. It does not establish whether a card payment succeeded or failed.
