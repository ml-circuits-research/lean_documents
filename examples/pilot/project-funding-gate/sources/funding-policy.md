# Project funding conditions

Meridian Private Capital · Harbour House redevelopment  
Case MPC-HH-2026-11 · Approved funding policy · 2 November 2026

## S01 — Conditions for the next tranche

The next project tranche is EUR 4 million. The sponsor must have contributed settled cash equal to at least half that amount. Pledges and unpaid commitments do not count. The matching condition is inclusive.

The tranche qualifies for immediate release if and only if all three conditions hold: the matching contribution requirement is met, both milestones M1 and M2 have been formally accepted by the project controller, and a release approval has been granted by an officer or committee with authority for the full tranche. These are the only release conditions and the only applicable route. There is no waiver of the milestone conditions.
