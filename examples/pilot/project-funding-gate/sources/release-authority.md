## S03 — Approval powers and supplied decisions

The project director may approve a full tranche of no more than EUR 3 million, inclusive. A tranche above EUR 3 million requires an investment board approval. The authority ceiling applies to the entire tranche, not to individual instalments. Neither authority may waive the milestone conditions in the funding policy.

The director signed a request on 5 November asking the board to approve the EUR 4 million tranche. The request is not a board decision. No board decision is included in this supplied correspondence. The correspondence is not an exhaustive history of board decisions held or circulated elsewhere, so it does not establish whether board approval has been granted.
