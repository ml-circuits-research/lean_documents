# The House at Low Tide

## S01 — First stanza

I keep a light beside the kitchen door.  
I keep the cup you left beneath the stair.  
The tide lays down its keys upon the shore.  
A chair remembers how to hold the air.

## S02 — Second stanza

I keep the orchard folded in a seed.  
I keep the clock that cannot name the day.  
The windows fill with boats I cannot read.  
Their small departures carry blue away.

## S03 — Third stanza

I keep no map of where the river ends.  
I keep no promise that the walls will stay.  
Outside, the rain rehearses being friends.  
Inside, your cup is waiting for the day.
