# Agreed leverage test

Alder Family Capital · Acquisition of Westhaven Packaging  
Case AFC-WHP-2026-11 · Financing terms · 5 November 2026

## S02 — Maximum leverage

For the proposed closing, net senior debt equals senior acquisition debt less unrestricted target cash. The maximum permitted net senior leverage is 3.0 times the stipulated annual EBITDA, inclusive. The proposal satisfies this covenant if and only if net senior debt is no greater than three times that EBITDA. There is no rounding allowance, grace margin or applicable covenant waiver.

Meeting this numeric test would not by itself grant final lender credit approval. That approval is a separate decision by the lender's credit committee.
