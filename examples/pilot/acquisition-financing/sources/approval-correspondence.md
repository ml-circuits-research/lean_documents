## S03 — Arranger authority and committee decision

The lead arranger signed the proposed term sheet on 5 November 2026. Its mandate authorizes negotiation and circulation of proposed financing terms only. It does not authorize the arranger to grant final lender credit approval. The signed term sheet expressly remains subject to a credit committee decision.

No credit committee decision is included in this supplied file. The file is not a complete history of decisions held by the lender or communicated elsewhere. It therefore establishes neither that final lender credit approval was granted nor that it was refused or never occurred.
