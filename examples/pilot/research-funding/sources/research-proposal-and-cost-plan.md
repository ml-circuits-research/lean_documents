# ThermalLoop research proposal and cost plan

North Coast Materials Institute · Helix Process Systems · Solhaven Test Laboratory  
Proposal TL-2026-17 · Industrial Heat Storage Research Call · Cost plan submitted 6 November 2026

## S01 — Research scope and requested costs

The consortium proposes a twelve-month investigation of ceramic heat-storage modules for industrial waste-heat recovery. North Coast Materials Institute will develop the material, Helix Process Systems will build test modules, and Solhaven Test Laboratory will independently repeat the thermal-cycling measurements. The study aims to assess durability; it does not claim that commercial performance has already been demonstrated.

The complete proposed cost plan is below. Amounts are exact EUR amounts, include all project costs and contain no overlapping items. No additional taxes, overheads or other costs enter the funding calculation.

| Cost item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Research personnel | EUR 400,000 |
| Materials and prototype consumables | EUR 250,000 |
| Independent replication service | EUR 150,000 |
| Purchase of a general-purpose furnace | EUR 200,000 |
| Total proposed project cost | EUR 1,000,000 |

The furnace remains available for unrelated laboratory work after the project. It is a purchase of general-purpose equipment, not a consumable or a separately invoiced research service. The other three rows are the consortium's research personnel, project consumables and independent replication service respectively.
