# Industrial Heat Storage Research Call: funding and award rules

Call IHS-2026 · Research Programme Office · Rules applicable to proposal TL-2026-17 · 2 November 2026

## S02 — Eligible costs and unconditional award

The call funds at most 60% of eligible project costs. Research personnel, project materials and prototype consumables, and an independent replication service are eligible in full. Purchase of general-purpose equipment is entirely ineligible. These are the only cost categories applicable to this proposal. The proposed items are classified by their stated nature, without transferring an equipment purchase into another category. There is no additional cap, rounding allowance or special funding-rate exception. A calculated maximum contribution is not an awarded grant.

An unconditional award requires a completed independent replication of the thermal-cycling results and formal acceptance of that replication by the evaluation panel before the review cutoff. A planned service, a budget allocation or the originating laboratory's own measurements does not satisfy this requirement. No officer or panel may waive it under this call. A recommendation to continue subject to further evidence is not an unconditional award and does not establish that the mandatory condition has been met.

The programme also requires an affirmative ethics determination before an unconditional award. The replication condition remains mandatory irrespective of the ethics determination. This document states the supplied call's requirements, not rules for other programmes.
