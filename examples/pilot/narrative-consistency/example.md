# The Name on the Crate — a story in five chapters

## Description

Two sisters and a porter share a harbor warehouse. A register uses two names for one person, and a disputed sighting raises questions about location and testimony. Examine identity, consistency and an unexplained motive.

## Review question

Does the supplied story support the report’s count of people, location claims and interpretation of Mira’s silence?
