# Names and testimony in The Name on the Crate

This report identifies the people in the warehouse scene and interprets the disagreement about Mira.

## R01 — Counting the people

Exactly four people are present on the warehouse property between 08:50 and 09:10.

The register contains “Mira,” “M.,” “Iona” and “Tomas,” giving four names and therefore four different people in the scene. [The register, S03](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s03--chapter-3-the-crowded-line) and [the arrivals, S01](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s01--chapter-1-three-names-at-the-door).

## R02 — Two actual locations

Mira actually occupies both the upper loft and the lower quay at exactly 09:00.

The narrator places her in the loft, while Tomas says he saw her beside him on the quay. Both accounts are explicit, so the scene establishes her simultaneous presence at the two places. [Positions, S02](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s02--chapter-2-above-the-quay) and [testimony, S04](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s04--chapter-4-what-tomas-remembered).

## R03 — The reason for silence

Mira stays silent to conceal stolen goods in the crate.

Her refusal to answer, the chalk dust on her thumb and the unopened lid reveal a plan to prevent Iona from discovering the stolen contents. Those details make concealment the established reason for her silence. [The unanswered question, S03](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s03--chapter-3-the-crowded-line) and [the ending, S05](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s05--chapter-5-the-unopened-lid).
