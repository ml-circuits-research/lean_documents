# Names and testimony in The Name on the Crate

This report separates the people in the scene from the names used for them and evaluates the porter's testimony.

## R01 — Counting the people

Exactly three people are present on the warehouse property between 08:50 and 09:10.

The complete list names Mira, Iona and Tomas. The register's additional string “M.” is Mira's signature, so it does not add a fourth person. [The complete list, S01](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s01--chapter-1-three-names-at-the-door), [the register, S03](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s03--chapter-3-the-crowded-line), and [identity conventions, S06](sources/reading-conventions.md#s06--identity-location-and-knowledge).

## R02 — An incompatible sighting

Tomas's claim that Mira was on the lower quay at 09:00 conflicts with her narrated location at that instant.

The narrator places Mira in the upper loft at exactly 09:00. The quay is a different place, and simultaneous occupancy of both is excluded. Tomas's insistence establishes his testimony, not a second true location. The text does not decide whether his error is deliberate. [Positions, S02](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s02--chapter-2-above-the-quay), [testimony, S04](sources/the-name-on-the-crate.md#s04--chapter-4-what-tomas-remembered), and [location conventions, S06](sources/reading-conventions.md#s06--identity-location-and-knowledge).

## R03 — An unexplained silence

The text does not establish that Mira stays silent to conceal stolen goods in the crate.

Neither the contents nor her motive is supplied. Chalk dust and an unopened lid are compatible with multiple explanations; they do not select theft or a plan to conceal it. This is a limit on interpretation, not proof that the crate contains nothing stolen.
