# The Name on the Crate: note on the present edition

## S06 — Identity, location and knowledge

The account of the property between 08:50 and 09:10 is complete: Mira, Iona and Tomas are its only occupants during that interval. Mira and the signature “M.” designate one person. Iona and Tomas are distinct from her and from each other. References to their father and to the absent driver do not add occupants to that interval.

The positions at exactly 09:00 describe one instant on the same morning. The upper loft and lower quay are distinct places; a person cannot be in both at that instant. The third-person account places Mira in the loft, Iona in the ground-floor office and Tomas on the quay. Tomas's later words concern that same Mira and instant. His speech is retained as testimony rather than a replacement for the narrated positions. It can be mistaken or deliberately false; the text does not decide which.

The contents of the crate and the reason for Mira's silence are not disclosed anywhere in this edition. The correction in the register is distinguished from the marks on the lid: Mira's abbreviated signature accompanies the former, while the story leaves the authorship of the latter unexplained. Touching chalk supplies no further account of the contents or her purpose. The scene ends with the lid unopened and offers no later disclosure.
