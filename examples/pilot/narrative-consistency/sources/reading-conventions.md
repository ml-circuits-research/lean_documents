# Reading conventions for The Name on the Crate

## S06 — Identity, location and knowledge

For counting the people on the property in the stated interval, use the narrator's explicit complete list in S01. Name strings are not automatically distinct people: “Mira” and “M.” designate the same individual. Iona and Tomas are distinct from Mira and from each other.

At exactly 09:00 the upper loft and lower quay are different locations, and a person cannot occupy both at that same instant. Direct narrative statements establish the scene's facts. Character dialogue establishes that a statement was made; it does not override those facts. Thus an incompatible sighting may be a mistake or a lie, without establishing which. No supplied passage identifies the crate's contents or the motive for Mira's silence.
