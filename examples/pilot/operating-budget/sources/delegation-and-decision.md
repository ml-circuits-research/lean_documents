## S03 — Recommendation and formal approval

The IT operations manager may recommend a purchase, but may not issue the finance director's formal approval. The finance director is the designated decision maker for a request up to EUR 25,000 including VAT, inclusive. This authority does not permit changing the annual allocation or excluding any cost required by the allocation policy. A recommendation, a budget calculation and a formal approval are separate records.

The IT operations manager signed a recommendation to proceed with IT-2027-014 on 5 November. It requests the finance director's decision; it is not that decision. No decision signed by the finance director is included in this supplied record. This record contains the correspondence forwarded to procurement, not a complete history of decisions held or sent elsewhere. It therefore does not establish whether the finance director has issued formal approval. A decision issued elsewhere would not change the stated allocation or the arithmetic test for whether this request fits it.
