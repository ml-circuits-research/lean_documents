# Reading the clock in The Last Crossing

This report examines the walk's chronology and Mara's response to the watchman.

## R01 — Length of the absence

Mara's absence from the ferry office lasts 25 minutes.

She leaves at 20:20 and returns at 20:55, so the interval between those two clock readings is 25 minutes. This measures her entire absence from the office. [Departure, S01](sources/the-last-crossing.md#s01--chapter-1-the-clock-above-the-door), [return, S03](sources/the-last-crossing.md#s03--chapter-3-two-cups), and [time conventions, S06](sources/reading-conventions.md#s06--time-and-attribution).

## R02 — A consistent recollection

Elias's claimed 20:10 return time is compatible with the established chronology of Mara's single walk.

His reference to the news gives a precise time for the same evening, and Mara does not correct him. His account can therefore be accepted alongside the 20:20 departure and 20:55 return as a consistent timing of that single walk. [The recollection, S04](sources/the-last-crossing.md#s04--chapter-4-the-watchmans-news), [departure, S01](sources/the-last-crossing.md#s01--chapter-1-the-clock-above-the-door), and [return, S03](sources/the-last-crossing.md#s03--chapter-3-two-cups).

## R03 — The purpose of silence

Mara remains silent because she intends to deceive Elias about her return time.

By leaving his incorrect recollection unchallenged, she conceals the true chronology from him. Her later movement of the key confirms that this silence is a calculated deception rather than uncertainty or reluctance to speak.
