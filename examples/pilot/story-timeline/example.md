# The Last Crossing — a story in five chapters

## Description

A woman crosses a river to a locked workshop. A watchman later gives a return time that differs from the narrated sequence. Examine elapsed time, the consistency of testimony and the limits of reading intention from silence.

## Review question

Does the supplied story support the report’s chronology and interpretation of Mara’s silence?
