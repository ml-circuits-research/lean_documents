# Reading conventions for The Last Crossing

## S06 — Time and attribution

All clock times in the story use the same correctly running local clock and the same calendar evening. Read 20:20 and 20:55 as exact minute values. Elapsed minutes are the return time minus the departure time. The walk's return must occur after its departure; there is no midnight crossing or second departure to resolve a reversed order.

Statements made directly by the third-person narration establish events for this textual analysis. Quoted statements establish what a character said, not automatically the truth of the statement's content. A contradiction between a character's recollection and an established event does not establish deliberate deception. These conventions do not provide a hidden motive, a future outcome or an authorial explanation for Mara's silence.
