# The Last Crossing: note on the present edition

## S06 — Time and attribution

This edition uses one correctly running local clock and one calendar evening throughout. The departure at 20:20 and return at 20:55 are exact minute readings, not approximate expressions. Mara makes a single walk from the office to the workshop and back. The elapsed office-to-office interval includes her stops and is measured by subtracting departure from return. There is no midnight transition or additional departure, and a return follows its departure.

The third-person account gives the events of this edition. Elias's dialogue is printed as his recollection, not as a replacement for the narrated return. His reference is to the same walk on the same evening. A discrepancy in a recollection leaves open both mistake and deliberate falsehood; the discrepancy alone does not choose between them.

The text gives Mara's passing thought about the workshop lamp. It supplies no explanation of her later silence or movement of the key, no hidden motive, and no continuation revealing what she does next. This note does not resolve those omissions.
