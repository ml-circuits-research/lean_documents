# Proposed investment terms

Northbridge Growth Fund · Cedar Components  
Case NB-CED-2026-11 · Deal team term sheet · 4 November 2026

## S01 — Transaction terms

The proposed transaction is a subscription by Northbridge Growth Fund for newly issued equity in Cedar Components. The agreed figures for the proposal are shown below.

| Term | Proposed amount or interest |
| --- | --- |
| Cash subscription by the fund | EUR 8 million |
| Fund ownership immediately after the subscription | 20% of total issued equity |
| Subscription type | Newly issued equity only |

The subscription price is proportional to ownership of the entire equity. The post-money equity valuation is the cash subscription divided by the fund’s ownership percentage immediately after the subscription. All figures are exact. There is no secondary purchase, fee, debt adjustment or other consideration affecting this calculation. The terms describe a proposal, not a completed investment or an approval decision.
