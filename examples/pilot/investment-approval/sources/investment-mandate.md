# Investment Committee decision

Northbridge Growth Fund · Cedar Components  
Case NB-CED-2026-11 · Approved minutes · 3 November 2026

## S02 — Approved allocation

The Investment Committee approves a maximum fund allocation of EUR 6 million to Cedar Components. The limit is inclusive: the proposed cash investment must not exceed EUR 6 million to be within this allocation. The limit applies to the full proposed subscription, not to a first instalment or a percentage of it.

The committee considered the EUR 8 million proposal and approved only the EUR 6 million allocation. Its approved decision grants no waiver, increase or exception permitting the proposed EUR 8 million subscription. A new committee decision would be required to increase this allocation. These minutes record the operative allocation decision for the proposal under review.
