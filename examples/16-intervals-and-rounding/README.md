# Is the service bill calculated correctly?

A service incident includes overlapping pauses, invoice adjustments and a separate credit note. Check a settlement report’s time calculations and rounding.

**Question:** Does the report count paused time once and round each charge under the stated rule?

Upload a service policy, operations ledger and invoice amounts. Choose either [the careful report](report-correct.md) or [the report with deliberate errors](report.md) and audit it separately against all files in [sources/](sources/). No separate brief is needed; derive the questions from the report and source rules.

The audit can check overlapping pauses, exact deadline boundaries, signed rounding and invoice versus credit-note scope. It reviews the supplied report, including its reasons and citations; it does not write the original report.

No prior-quarter incident history or definition of reasonable compensation is supplied. The stated rules cannot answer those questions by themselves.

These are fictional examples. Keep this guide, the alternate report, metadata, generation files and evaluator material outside the audit input. Evaluator expectations are withheld during a blind audit; the selected report and source documents are always available.

Historical execution records are indexed under [validation/runs](../../validation/runs/). This guide does not constitute a new validation run. Original reports and sources retain their bytes so they can be matched to prior results. New runs belong under `tmp/audit-runs/`, with a separate directory for each report.
