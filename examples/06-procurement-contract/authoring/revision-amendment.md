# HPS revision transition and requisition schedule

All organizations, people, agreements, events, and rules in this source are synthetic.

## [V01]

Revision 2 takes effect at 2029-06-01 00:00 UTC. An accepted requisition received before that instant remains under revision 1 for its original scope, even if approval, supplier selection, commitment, delivery, or invoicing occurs later. An accepted requisition received at the exact boundary or afterward uses revision 2. Accepted receipt is the central intake service's timestamp, not the time when an employee drafted an email or a buyer opened a task. This amendment changes the threshold but does not retroactively invalidate approvals valid under the retained revision. A dashboard field showing the presently published standard is not a record of which version governs an individual requisition.

## [V02]

A material scope amendment accepted on or after 2029-06-01 is governed by revision 2 only for the additional independently priced requirement introduced by that amendment. It does not erase competition obligations for the unchanged original requirement. A change is material when it adds a distinct functional requirement or increases unavoidable consideration; correcting a contact name or an internal billing code is not material. If an original and added requirement satisfy the aggregation rule, the buyer must document the aggregation analysis rather than presume that a new request number prevents it. The files for the two grandfathering examples state expressly that neither contains an added requirement, a price increase, or a genuine material amendment.

## [V03]

Accepted requisition R-17 concerns one instrument purchase at EUR 22,500.00. Intake receipt occurred at 2029-05-31 23:59 UTC. A June 3 clerical amendment changed the internal contact from the field technician to the warehouse supervisor and corrected a cost-center spelling. The approved schedule states that the specification, supplier, price, quantity, and functional requirement remained unchanged. Commitment occurred on June 10. The amendment did not create an additional purchase and does not constitute a material scope amendment under V02. The current-version dashboard displayed revision 2 on the day of commitment because revision 2 was then the standard's published version.

## [V04]

Accepted requisition R-18 concerns one distinct instrument purchase at EUR 22,500.00. Central intake receipt occurred at 2029-06-01 00:00 UTC. The receipt is exact to the minute and is not a rounded display of an earlier time. Commitment occurred on June 10. No other purchase in this dossier belongs to its operational requirement, and no material amendment or aggregation relationship is recorded. The amount is already normalized in euros, excluding recoverable VAT and including freight. The boundary timestamp, rather than the identical commitment day or identical value shared with R-17, determines the applicable revision for this original requirement.

## [V05]

The requisition currency schedule fixes USD-to-EUR at exactly 0.900000 euros per US dollar for P-13 and CHF-to-EUR at exactly 1.040000 euros per Swiss franc for P-22. These are synthetic contractual rates for this assessment and are not claims about an actual exchange market. P-13 permits a transaction amount of USD 27,777.775, has no freight charge, and has no unconditional rebate or separately included VAT. P-22 specifies CHF 18,000.00 equipment plus CHF 1,000.00 unavoidable freight. Its possible year-end loyalty credit is discretionary and is not an unconditional rebate at commitment. Later accounting software may display only two currency decimals, but those displays do not amend these exact contractual inputs.

## [V06]

The transition package contains the full operative standard, this amendment, the named purchase register, delegation notice, exception file, attestation scans, and reconciliation correspondence. Annex Q was not supplied. The transition package does not contain a separate Annex Q deadline, compatibility checklist, certification rule, or written interpretation adopted by the director. In particular, the administrative phrase “all attachments transferred except Q” is a complete inventory statement about this package; it is not an assertion that no employee holds Annex Q or that no procurement event occurred outside the explicitly complete transaction coverage. This distinction remains relevant when a report attempts to infer event absence from a missing attachment.
