# Parcel delivery record

[S1] For fictional parcel P19, the agreed latest physical delivery time is 17:00 local time on 4 November 2026, inclusive. There is no grace period. A parcel is on time exactly when its recorded physical delivery is no later than that deadline.
[S2] The signed receiving record states physical delivery of P19 at 17:12 local time on 4 November 2026. The same record states dispatch at 15:00 that day. Dispatch is distinct from physical delivery. These timestamps are exact and use the same clock.
[S3] The receiving signature acknowledges receipt of the parcel only. No inspection of its contents, damage report or item-condition statement is supplied. The record does not establish whether the contents were damaged or intact.
