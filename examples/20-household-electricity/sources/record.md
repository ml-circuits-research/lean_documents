# Household meter and tariff record

[S1] The fictional household meter read exactly 1200 kWh at the start of the billing period and 1350 kWh at the end. No reset or rollover occurred. Billed usage equals end reading minus start reading.
[S2] Every billed kWh costs exactly 20 cents. The sole additional charge is a fixed 500-cent period fee. These are final amounts; no tax, discount, adjustment or other charge applies. Total charge equals usage multiplied by the per-kWh price, plus the fixed fee.
[S3] This packet contains meter and tariff information only. It includes no bank statement, payment receipt or supplier account balance and does not establish whether the bill has been paid.
