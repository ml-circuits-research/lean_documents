[M05] The maintenance package is complete for MA-27 and MB-27 window beginnings, endings, authorized operations, and priority restrictions. It includes no other window relevant to incidents A, B, or C during the assessment interval. This window completeness does not make the monitoring data complete and does not certify incident containment. The two authorizations have different scopes despite overlapping calendar dates and a shared service operator. MA-27 cannot be transferred to B simply because it ends near B’s window, and MB-27 cannot excuse a P1 action for A. A document reference bearing only the word maintenance, without the operation identifier and time interval, is not sufficient to select the proper clause.
