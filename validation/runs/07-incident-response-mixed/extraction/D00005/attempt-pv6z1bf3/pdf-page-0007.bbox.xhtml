[M06] An operations margin note says that analysts sometimes refer informally to the maintenance interval as a quiet period. The signed authorization expressly states that this phrase has no additional technical meaning. It does not suspend emergency notifications, authorize silent handling of security incidents, or establish that all customer traffic was absent. The signed priority restriction and effective-clock clauses take precedence over this informal shorthand. There is no clause requiring a reviewer to infer a favorable outcome whenever work was planned. The purpose of the package is to specify exactly which response- clock exception is authorized and to prevent unrelated windows or priorities from being combined.
