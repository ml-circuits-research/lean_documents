# Model meaning, input provenance and limits

The model concerns fictional Harbor Procurement Standard transactions during May-June 2029. All source facts are documentary assertions used under explicit source completeness. No external laws, exchange rates or procurement customs enter the model. Exact evidence and passage coordinates are in findings.json and the indexed corpus. Gregorian date/minute conversion and rational calculations were independently executed with Python datetime/Fraction and retained in arithmetic-check.md.

Money is integer euro cents after normalization. `p13Exact = 27777775 * 9` represents (27,777.775 USD = 27777775/1000) times (0.900000 EUR/USD = 9/10), with denominator 10000 euros. Thus 249999975 denotes EUR 24,999.9975. Inputs are V05 PDF page 6, visually verified, corroborated by F01; P04 DOCX paragraph 10 controls the operation. `roundTenThousandthsToCents n = (n+50)/100` is nearest-cent, half-up rounding for nonnegative exact ten-thousandths. `p14Cents` starts from 249999940 ten-thousandths = EUR 24,999.994, supported by L03/F02. No intermediate rounding occurs. The two values are separate nonaggregating requirements per L03/F02.

`revision` uses accepted-receipt minutes relative to 2029-06-01 00:00 UTC: a negative minute is revision 1, zero or later revision 2. V01 is PDF page 2. `threshold` maps revision 1 to 2000000 cents and revision 2 to 2500000 cents from P02 DOCX paragraph 6; only these two version numbers occur in instantiated models. `competitionRequired` is inclusive threshold comparison before applying an exception. R-17 receipt -1 and unchanged original scope are V03 page 4; V02 page 3 distinguishes its clerical amendment from a material added requirement. R-18 receipt 0 is V04 page 5. Approval, assignment, amendment or commitment date is not substituted for receipt.

`rackAggregates` instantiates the three true shared-identity predicates: project TIDE-4, legal supplier Pelican Instruments Ltd, and one sensor-rack operational requirement (L02/F04). Its date comparison uses June 3 day offset 2 and June 20 offset 19, with inclusive last date first+29 from P03 DOCX paragraph 8. The actual gap is 17 days and the second order is on window day 18. `rackKnownBeforeFirst` records the May 28 specification's complete prior knowledge. `rackCents` sums normalized 1240000 and 1260000 cents (L02). Revision 2 derives from June 1 09:00 accepted requisition. Different pallets and cost centers do not rebut P03. This model is for that named pair, not an unbounded proof about all possible purchases.

`delegatedApproval` is restricted to events represented by nonnegative June-1-origin timestamps, thus within or after delegation commencement. It checks covered category, value at most 1500000 cents, approval minute at most 21180, and strict approval-before-commitment. D01-D03 establish category, inclusive monetary limit, inclusive June 15 17:00 expiry and exact-minute convention; D02 establishes no extension. L04 gives P-15 value 1490000, approval 21180, commitment 21181, and P-16 value 1500000, approval 21181, commitment 21190. Both are laboratory consumables. No alternative P-16 approval exists within complete L01/L04 coverage. The expiry condition is applied at approval time, not commitment time.
