[V01] Revision 2 takes effect at 2029-06-01 00:00 UTC. An accepted requisition received before that instant remains under revision 1 for its original scope, even if approval, supplier selection, commitment, delivery, or invoicing occurs later. An accepted requisition received at the exact boundary or afterward uses revision 2. Accepted receipt is the central intake service’s timestamp, not the time when an employee drafted an email or a buyer opened a task. This amendment changes the threshold but does not retroactively invalidate approvals valid under the retained revision. A dashboard field showing the presently published standard is not a record of which version governs an individual requisition.
