# Audit brief

<a id="B01"></a>
## Scope
Audit one supplied report variant per run: PA-01 through PA-10 in `report.md`, or PA-C01 through PA-C10 in `report-correct.md`, against the supplied records for operations C-101 through C-105. Assess policy compliance only under fictional Policy P-17. No external law, organizational practice, or undocumented facts may be assumed. The records stop at 2028-04-12 17:00 UTC.

<a id="B02"></a>
## Time and decision conventions
All timestamps are UTC, use the Gregorian calendar, and have exact minute precision; none abbreviate an uncertain time interval. “Before” is strict (<); simultaneous events do not satisfy it. “Within 24 hours after start” includes both endpoints, so a countersignature exactly 24 hours after start is timely. A request is not an approval. An incident qualification and declaration are distinct facts.

<a id="B03"></a>
## Evidence boundaries
Only register coverage expressly stated in R01 permits inference from absence. Explicit “none” entries are negative evidence within that coverage. Missing material outside that coverage supports neither a positive nor a negative assertion. Operator attestations do not override the register or each other; conflicting statements about the same proposition must remain visible. Derive supported, refuted, both, or undetermined status from the supplied assertions and permitted rules, not from imagined ground truth. A qualitative judgment without an operational definition remains outside formal assessment.

<a id="B04"></a>
## Questions
For each claim: identify relevant source anchors; distinguish standard and exception paths; preserve time boundaries; distinguish explicit negative entries, missing evidence, and contradictory evidence; state which assessment could prospectively be proved or refuted under explicit assumptions and which is blocked or nonformal. Do not run Lean for this preparation fixture.

The selected supplied report is always visible to the auditor and is the object being audited. Creating a substantive report from the sources is outside this fixture. Evaluator-only expected outcomes remain separate and hidden during blind assessment. Neither supplied report is an authoritative evidence source for the other.
