# Synthetic narrative continuity brief

<a id="B-01"></a>
## B-01 — Questions
Audit each numbered claim in the selected supplied report (`report.md` or `report-correct.md`) against the four source documents. Determine whether each asserted event or state fits the world chronology, whether quoted speech establishes a world fact, whether a flashback changes story time, whether resurrection is permitted by the stated rules, and whether an aesthetic evaluation is formally assessable.

<a id="B-02"></a>
## B-02 — Time and scope
Days are integer story dates within the bounded interval day 1 through day 10. Events marked “dawn” take effect before events marked “noon” or “evening” on that day. Source chronology is authoritative for this interval. The resurrection catalog in rules R-02 is explicitly complete for Mira in this interval; other people and times are outside scope. Physical attendance at a meeting requires being alive at the time of attendance. A remembered scene is located at its explicitly labeled story date, independently of the date on which it is narrated.

<a id="B-03"></a>
## B-03 — Evidence levels
Distinguish authoritative world records, quoted character speech, and evaluative opinion. A statement being uttered does not establish its truth. The selected report is the object under review, not a new canonical scene; the other report variant is excluded from that audit. Source silence is not a negative fact except for the expressly complete resurrection catalog and interval rules. A ghost, dream, impersonator, or metaphor does not count as physical attendance. Use only supplied sources; do not invent off-page repairs. This is a fixture review, not an authorized Lean run.
