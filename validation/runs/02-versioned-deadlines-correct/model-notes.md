# Model interpretation

The model covers fictional W-6 only, for cases D-201 through D-204. The source inputs are transcriptions of documentary facts and rules, not independently authenticated events. `Case.received` is the intake accepted receipt, never the internal assignment. `Case.kind` is the explicit classification in C01-C04. `submitted` records an actual submission, making the timeliness assessments non-vacuous. `assigned` is retained to demonstrate that it does not affect revision selection.

Time is integer UTC minutes since 2028-02-20 00:00. Receipt date is extracted by integer division by 1440. The deadline adds 10 dates for revision 1 or 7 for revision 2, then sets time to minute 1020 (17:00). This implements V1-02/V2-02 and B02 day-zero inclusive convention. No weekend, holiday or business-day adjustment is included. Gregorian date-to-offset conversion is external to Lean and was independently executed using Python datetime; complete results and day-addition checks are in calendar-check.md. The leap date 2028-02-29 is included. An additional C04 assignment at 2028-03-02 11:00 UTC is offset 16500; its value is not used in any deadline calculation.
