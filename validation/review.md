## Substantive reasoning checks

1. **Prior approval and emergency rules:** The four covered operations use explicit register negatives. C-105 has no fabricated operation record. Declaration, incident qualification, countersignature, and standard approval retain their separate conditions. The countersignature endpoint is exactly 1440 minutes after start and passes the inclusive test.
2. **Dates and versions:** Calendar translation remains an acknowledged source-mapping step outside Lean. The offsets agree with February 20 + 10 days = March 1, February 29 + 10 days = March 10, and March 1 + 7 days = March 8 in 2028. Neither assignment dates nor the export label change applicability. Missing Annex R is represented as inapplicability/unavailability of the standard rule, never as a known absent deadline.
3. **Arithmetic and population scope:** The 20-entry population has two known exceptions and 16 unknown statuses, permitting 2–18 exceptions. An exact 50% population rate remains possible but not established. Lower-bound proofs expose the source-based sample-membership decomposition instead of claiming to authenticate it.
4. **Narrative time and state:** The conditional attendance targets do not assume nonattendance. They derive it from a sourced necessary condition and an independently supported dead state. Positive death evidence is sufficient to refute the universal never-died claim without a complete death catalog. Negative revival conclusions use the expressly complete bounded revival catalog. The time encodings are coordinates for named instants; they are not measurements of dawn clock time or exhaustive continuous-time histories. The chosen day-8-noon witnesses lie inside the actual report interval.
5. **Conflicting evidence:** QA/Operations data encode attributed assertions with matching scope, not unconditional P and not-P world axioms. There is no explosion from contradictory premises and no implied source precedence from later publication.
6. **Support gaps:** Countermodel/completion results are labeled auxiliary when the report asserts an unresolved underlying event. In the correct report, a direct proof of a non-entailment meta-claim is appropriate. The findings explain these distinctions rather than relying on labels alone. Signature authenticity, the act of historical verification, marker presence, and supplied verification material are not presented as interchangeable facts.
7. **Explanation completeness:** Findings expose exact source-linked report wording, the verification question, evidence quotes/anchors, rules, assumptions, steps, limits, and a recommendation. Atomic splits retain the full parent paragraph and use the question to identify the checked part. A reader should use that question and exact target rather than interpret a proof for one atomic finding as certification of every sentence in the displayed parent quote.

The observed small fixture success is not evidence of general reliability on arbitrary reports or formats.
